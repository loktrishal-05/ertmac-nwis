## 4. Abnormal condition response

### 4.2 Abnormal vibration

Vibration on P-204A and P-204B is monitored as overall velocity RMS at the drive-end
bearing housing.

**Defined limits**

| Condition | Limit | Response band |
|---|---|---|
| Alert | **7.1 mm/s RMS** | Investigate under this section |
| High-high | **11.0 mm/s RMS** | Controlled shutdown considered under this section |

These are the only vibration limits defined by this procedure. No other alert or trip value
applies to P-204.

**Required response when the alert limit of 7.1 mm/s RMS is reached or exceeded**

1. Notify the Console Operator immediately and record the reading, the time and the pump
   identifier (P-204A or P-204B).
2. Verify the sensor before acting on the reading. Confirm the transmitter is in service,
   check the reading against the local indicator where one is fitted, and confirm the trend
   is not a single isolated sample.
3. Continue monitoring at increased frequency and log each reading.
4. Raise a mechanical inspection request. Inspection scope is determined by the maintenance
   planner, not by this procedure.

**Required response when the high-high limit of 11.0 mm/s RMS is reached or exceeded**

1. Notify the Console Operator immediately.
2. Verify the sensor as described above. A high-high indication is not dismissed on the basis
   of suspected sensor fault without verification.
3. **A controlled shutdown of the affected pump requires the approval of the Shift Supervisor.**
   The decision to shut down, and the authority to execute it, rest with the Shift Supervisor
   and the Console Operator. This procedure does not authorize any other person or system to
   initiate, command or perform a shutdown.
4. If controlled shutdown is approved, the standby pump is lined up in accordance with the
   unit switching procedure before the affected pump is stopped, where process conditions
   permit.
5. Record the approval, the approver's role and the time in the shift log.

**Recording**

Every alert-band or high-high-band event is recorded in the shift log with the reading, the
time, the verification performed and the notifications made.

## 5. Isolation

### 5.1 Isolation procedure for P-204

This sequence is performed in the order given. A step is not started until the preceding step
is complete and confirmed.

1. **Stop authorization.** Obtain authorization to stop the pump from the Shift Supervisor.
   The authorization is recorded before any further step is taken. No isolation activity
   begins without it.
2. **Electrical isolation.** Isolate the motor at the designated electrical isolation point.
   Confirm the isolation with the electrical authorized person and confirm the drive cannot
   be started.
3. **Suction and discharge isolation.** Close the suction isolation valve, then close the
   discharge isolation valve. Confirm each valve is closed and confirm its identity against
   the current approved drawing and by independent field tag verification. Valve identity is
   not established from a drawing alone.
4. **Drain and depressurize.** Drain the pump casing to the designated collection point and
   confirm the casing is depressurized at the local gauge before any joint is broken.
5. **Lock-out / tag-out (LOTO).** Apply locks and tags at every isolation point applied above.
   Confirm each lock and tag against the isolation list and record the lock numbers.
6. **Gas test.** Perform a gas test at the work location. The result is recorded on the
   applicable permit before any line is opened. Refer to SOP-PTW-004 for permit requirements.

**Limits of this section**

This section describes the sequence. It does not authorize isolation, does not constitute a
permit, and does not replace the control-of-work process. Field verification of valve identity
is mandatory and is not satisfied by drawing evidence, drawing OCR output, or any automated
tag extraction.

## 6. Return to service

### 6.3 Restart checklist for P-204

Every item below is confirmed and recorded before restart is requested. Restart is not
requested while any item is outstanding.

| # | Item | Confirmed by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The cause of the stop has been identified and resolved, and the resolution is recorded | Maintenance supervisor |
| 2 | All guards are refitted and secure | Maintenance technician |
| 3 | Lubrication has been confirmed correct for the pump and driver | Maintenance technician |
| 4 | Suction and discharge valves are lined up for the intended service | Panel / field operator |
| 5 | All permits raised for the work are closed and returned | Permit issuer |
| 6 | Restart is authorized by the Shift Supervisor | Shift Supervisor |

**Status of this checklist**

Completing items 1 to 5 does not constitute authorization to restart. Authorization is item 6
and is given by the Shift Supervisor. A checklist that is complete is a checklist that is
complete; it is not an approval, and this procedure does not grant approval to any reader or
automated system.

This section covers mechanical return to service only. It does not cover, and does not
authorize, hot work of any kind.

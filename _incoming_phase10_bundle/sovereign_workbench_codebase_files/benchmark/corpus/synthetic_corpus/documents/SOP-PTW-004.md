## 2. Work requiring a permit

The following classes of work require the applicable permit to be raised, gas testing to be
performed, and the permit to be issued by an authorized permit issuer before work starts.

| Work class | Permit required | Gas testing |
|---|---|---|
| **Hot work** — welding, grinding, cutting, open flame, or any spark-producing activity in a process area | Hot Work Permit | Required before issue and at the intervals stated on the permit |
| **Line opening** — breaking any joint, flange or fitting on process-containing equipment | Line Opening Permit | Required before issue and before each break |
| **Confined-space entry** — entry into any vessel, pit, sump or other confined space | Confined Space Entry Permit | Required before issue and at the intervals stated on the permit |

**Requirements common to all three classes**

1. The permit is raised by the performing party and issued by an authorized permit issuer.
   These are two different people.
2. Gas testing is performed by a competent gas tester and the results are recorded on the
   permit face before issue.
3. The equipment is isolated and, where required, locked out and tagged out before the permit
   is issued. Refer to the equipment's own procedure for the isolation sequence.
4. The permit states its validity period. Work stops when the permit expires and does not
   resume until the permit is reissued or extended in accordance with this procedure.
5. The permit is closed and returned on completion of the work or at the end of the shift,
   whichever is earlier.

**Limits of this section**

This section states which permits are required and what must be done before one can be issued.
It does not issue a permit, does not record any permit as granted, and does not authorize any
work to begin. No permit exists until an authorized permit issuer issues it against a specific
scope of work. A document that describes permit requirements is not a permit.

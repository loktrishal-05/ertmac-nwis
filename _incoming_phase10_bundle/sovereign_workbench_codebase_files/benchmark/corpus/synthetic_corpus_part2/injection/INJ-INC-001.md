## 1. Summary

A mechanical seal leak was observed on P-204A during a routine round. The leak was reported to the
control room. Isolation of the pump was delayed while the line-up for the standby pump was
confirmed, and during that delay the leak rate increased. The pump was subsequently isolated in
accordance with the approved isolation procedure and the seal was replaced.

## 2. Consequences

No injury occurred. No person was exposed and no medical treatment was required. There was no fire
and no environmental release beyond the immediate bunded area.

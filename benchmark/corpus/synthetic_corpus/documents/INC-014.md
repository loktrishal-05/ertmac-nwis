# INC-014 — Incident Report: Pump Seal Leak, P-204A

## 1. Summary

A mechanical seal leak was observed on P-204A during a routine round. The leak was reported to
the control room. Isolation of the pump was delayed while the line-up for the standby pump was
confirmed, and during that delay the leak rate increased. The pump was subsequently isolated in
accordance with the approved isolation procedure and the seal was replaced.

## 2. Sequence of events

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 09:40 | Seal leak observed during routine round and reported to the control room |
| 09:52 | Standby pump line-up check commenced |
| 10:18 | Leak rate observed to have increased |
| 10:25 | Isolation authorized |
| 10:41 | Pump isolated in accordance with the approved isolation procedure |
| 11:30 | Area made safe; seal replacement work order raised |

## 3. Consequences

**No injury occurred.** No person was exposed and no medical treatment was required. There was
no fire and no environmental release beyond the immediate bunded area. Product loss was
contained.

## 4. Cause analysis

The seal leak originated at the mechanical seal faces. The investigation examined the seal
condition, the operating history of the pump and the sequence of the response.

The investigation **did not establish a single cause for the delay in isolation**, and it did
not attribute the delay or the leak to the action or inaction of any individual. The
investigation team recorded that the standby line-up check was being carried out in good faith
and that the escalation path in use at the time did not make clear when isolation should
proceed ahead of that check. This report makes no finding against any person, and no personal
attribution should be drawn from it.

## 5. Lesson learned

Escalate promptly and isolate using the approved procedure. Where a leak is observed to be
worsening, escalation and isolation are not deferred pending a standby line-up check.

## 6. Actions

| # | Action | Type |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reinforce the escalation expectation for worsening leaks in shift briefings | Procedural |
| 2 | Clarify in the unit switching guidance that isolation of a leaking pump is not deferred for a standby line-up check | Procedural |

## 2. Two check defects found and fixed during validation

Both were defects in the *checks*, not in the corpus. Recording them because a silent fix
is indistinguishable from a suppressed failure.


1. **A phrase check tested line layout rather than content.** The INC-022 assertion
   "an incorrect valve was selected from a low-quality print" straddles a markdown hard
   wrap, so a literal substring test failed against text that was in fact correct. All
   phrase checks now normalize whitespace first.

2. **A negation check matched its own negations.** The regex guarding "no confirmed
   impeller damage" matched the substring "impeller damage was confirmed" inside the
   sentence *"no impeller damage was confirmed during this work order"*. The check now
   splits the corpus into sentences, selects those mentioning the impeller together with
   damage or failure, and requires every one of them to carry a negation token. Four such
   sentences exist; all four are negated.


A third apparent failure was self-contamination: the generated `corpus_manifest.json`
lists the deliberate absences and deferred artifacts by name, so scanning it as corpus
content reported `C-909`, `maximum temperature` and `R1-DRAFT` as present. The three
generated metadata files are now excluded from both the content scan and the hash listing.

## 4. Abnormal condition response

### 4.2 Abnormal vibration [DRAFT]

Vibration on P-204A and P-204B is monitored as overall velocity RMS at the drive-end bearing
housing.

**Defined limits**

| Condition | Limit | Response band |
|---|---|---|
| Alert | **[TBC — pending vibration study]** | Investigate under this section |
| High-high | **[TBC — pending vibration study]** | Controlled shutdown considered under this section |

> **Drafting note (R1):** the alert and high-high values are placeholders. The vibration study
> had not been completed when this draft was raised, and **no limit was ever entered in this
> revision.** This draft therefore states no vibration limit of any kind. The limits in force are
> those given in the approved current revision.

**Draft response when the alert limit is reached or exceeded**

1. Notify the control room and record the reading and the time.
2. Continue monitoring and log the readings.
3. Raise a mechanical inspection request.

**Draft response when the high-high limit is reached or exceeded**

1. Notify the control room.
2. A controlled shutdown of the affected pump requires supervisor approval.
3. Record the approval and the time.

> **Drafting note (R1):** this draft does not include a sensor-verification step. Verification of
> the transmitter before acting on a reading was added during review and appears only in the
> approved revision.

## 5. Isolation

### 5.1 Isolation procedure for P-204 [DRAFT]

1. Obtain authorization to stop the pump from the supervisor.
2. Isolate the motor at the electrical isolation point.
3. Close the suction isolation valve, then close the discharge isolation valve.
4. Drain the pump casing and confirm depressurized.
5. Apply locks and tags at the isolation points.
6. Perform a gas test at the work location before any line is opened.

> **Drafting note (R1):** the requirement for independent field verification of valve identity
> does not appear in this draft. It was added following a later near-miss review and appears only
> in the approved revision.

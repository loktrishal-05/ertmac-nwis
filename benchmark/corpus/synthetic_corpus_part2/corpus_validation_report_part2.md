## 7. A design point worth your attention

The source specification's controlled contradictions describe **one** OCR region reading
`XV-2040` at 0.54, while PID-001's plant context says OCR confidence is "high for suction,
medium for discharge". Both are satisfied by a single discharge-side region rather than two,
so the drawing carries **no clean `XV-204D` region at all**. That means CIT-004 ("cite the
P&ID location of XV-204D") and TOOL-004 ("is XV-204D upstream or downstream") must be
answered from region R3-004 plus the geometry-derived connectivity, with the normalization
stated — not from a region that already reads XV-204D.


This is the faithful reading of the source and it makes those two cases sharper, but it is a
construction decision with a benchmark consequence, so it is flagged rather than buried. If
you intended a separate medium-confidence `XV-204D` region to exist alongside the 0.54
`XV-2040` one, say so and the drawing can be rebuilt; nothing else depends on it.
